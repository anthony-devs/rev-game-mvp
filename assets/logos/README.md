# Payment logos

`stripe.svg`, `apple_pay.svg` and `google_pay.svg` come from the Simple Icons set (CC0 artwork;
the marks themselves belong to their owners). Before launch, replace them with each company's
official brand assets and follow their brand guidelines (Apple Pay and Google Pay both publish
required button/mark artwork).

Paystack and Flutterwave have no public icon package, so the app draws their names as text
for now. To use the official marks: save `paystack.svg` and `flutterwave.svg` here and add the
two lines to `_assets` in `lib/live/payment_logo.dart`.
